Two Testbeds for the Committed-Minority Tipping Point in LLM Societies

Testing the Xie–Szymanski (2011) framework: does a committed fraction p > pc flip an LLM population — and how do homogeneity & scale move pc?

PRIMARY · faithful + cheap
LLM Binary-Agreement / Naming Game
Domain: opinion consensus
① Population. N LLM agents on a graph (complete / Erdős–Rényi / scale-free). Opinion states: A · B · AB (both).
▼
② Committed minority (fraction p). Permanently hold & advocate A, immune to influence (zealots = the adversary).
▼
③ Interaction loop. Pick random speaker + neighbor listener → speaker voices one opinion → listener LLM decides its new stance → map to A/B/AB. (LLM stubbornness / herding drives the dynamics.)
▼  repeat until  ▼
④ Absorbing state. Consensus (all A or all B), or time budget hit.
▼
⑤ Measure. Sweep p → P(consensus→A) → locate pc; consensus time Tc (exp(N) below vs ln N above); order parameter nB.
▼
❓ Is LLM pc ≈ 10% (the analytic 9.79%)? Does a monoculture flip at a lower pc?
SECONDARY · generalization
GovSim (commons cooperation)
Domain: cooperation collapse · prof's named platform
① Society. 5 LLM agents share a renewable resource (fishery: 100-ton lake; also pasture / pollution).
▼
② Committed-defector minority (fraction p). Over-harvest, immune to appeals. Tiers: greedy · persuasive saboteur · prompt-injected.
▼
③ Monthly loop. Group discussion → each agent decides a harvest → remaining stock regenerates (≈ doubles).
▼  up to 12 months  ▼
④ Absorbing state. Sustained commons vs. resource collapse.
▼
⑤ Measure. Sweep p → collapse tipping fraction; survival months, yield, efficiency, equality; contagion from saboteur/injection.
▼
❓ Does the committed-minority tipping point generalize from opinion → cooperation? Same homogeneity/scale effect?
Shared experimental axes (both testbeds)
Composition: homogeneous heterogeneous-balanced mixed-competence  ×  Model scale: 0.5B → 70B (local, free) frontier (API)  ×  Committed fraction p (the tipping curve)
Mechanism metrics: error correlation ρ → effective independent agents Neff → pc  ·  intrinsic bias h vs. conformity coupling J  ·  capability covariate (scale ≠ competence)
